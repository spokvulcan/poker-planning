A throwaway prototype for ticket #260. Cards land anywhere in 2D, proximity is the grouping gesture, zones are hints rather than columns. All in memory, nothing is saved, nothing is real.

Drive it in this order

  1. In Collect, drag two cards together. Watch a hull form, then drag one 40px away and watch it dissolve.
  2. Turn on Simulate 8 people (bottom left) and try to keep working. Watch the write counter.
  3. Advance to Group. Name a hull. Then Shift-click three loose cards and press G.
  4. Advance to Vote, spend six dots. Then Discuss — press N repeatedly and watch the viewport fly.
  5. Flip Flight to Cut in the discuss panel and press N again. Compare.

Pan: two-finger scroll, drag empty space, or Space+drag. Zoom: ⌘/Ctrl+scroll, pinch, or +/−. 0 fits everything. G groups the selection, U ungroups. N/P walk topics. Arrow keys nudge.

Built for a trackpad. Usable on touch.
