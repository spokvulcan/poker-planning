# Retro is the second ceremony of one toolkit, on one room route

**Status:** accepted — decided on [map #253](https://github.com/spokvulcan/poker-planning/issues/253) via [#268](https://github.com/spokvulcan/poker-planning/issues/268). Specified, not yet built. Amended by [ADR-0024](0024-a-retro-shows-facts-and-never-a-measure-of-the-team.md): the claims register also may not say that any retro number measures the team, and the dashboard's Retros list and the team page show **retro facts**, never analytics.

Adding a retrospective to a planning-poker product forces a positioning choice, and the research and teardown behind this map narrow it more than marketing instinct would. The category is commercially unproven — Retrium has twelve G2 reviews after eleven years, Parabol retracted a monetisation crackdown as "overly aggressive" — so the retro is sold as the **second ceremony of a toolkit** an audience already uses, never as a standalone bet. The evidence licenses exactly two claims nobody else in the category can make: that thinking in writing, in parallel, recovers what distributed teams lose to the meeting format (`docs/research/retrospective-effectiveness.md` §5.2), and that history kept permanently and free inverts the category's monetisation (`docs/research/retro-tool-teardown.md`, "Continuity, given away rather than sold"). Everything else the evidence explicitly does *not* license (§5.5), and the map has already ruled AI and a workspace product out of scope.

So the position is: **AgileKit is the free, open-source way for distributed Scrum teams to estimate and reflect in writing, everyone at once, with nothing forgotten between sprints.** The hero keeps its earned line and widens it — "Estimate and reflect, without the noise." The product noun is **Retro**; "retrospective" survives only in long-form copy and metadata, and "ceremony" never appears in the product.

The in-app consequence is that the retro is a second *room type* on the existing chassis, not a second product: it shares the room route, the join flow and the dashboard, and gets its own creation route only because the poker creation path's zero-friction promise must not acquire a picker step.

## Considered Options

- **A ceremony picker at `/room/new`** (rejected). Every poker user pays an extra step so the retro can be discovered; the retro is discovered on the homepage instead.
- **Separate `/retro/[roomId]` route** (rejected). Forks the join page, the share-link copy and the shell read for no user benefit. `rooms.get` already returns `roomType`; the page branches on it.
- **Creation only from a team page** (rejected). Kills the teamless anonymous retro, which is the fastest path for someone who has never seen AgileKit and the direct counterpart of poker's "no accounts required".
- **A separate `/retro` landing page** (rejected). Two front doors say two products. One homepage, one `/features` page with two anchored sections.
- **A client-side retro demo, as ADR-0003 does for poker** (rejected for v1). A teamless retro is as instant as a simulation and costs nothing to create, so the CTA goes to the real thing. A second simulation reducer is a build-sized commitment for a page whose job the create button already does.
- **A dashboard organised by object (Rooms / Teams / Analytics)** (rejected). A signed-in person's home should name both ceremonies. "Sessions" was poker rooms wearing a generic hat.

## Consequences

- **Routes.** `/room/new` stays poker and keeps its metadata; `/retro/new` creates a retro (name, format defaulted, team optional). `/room/[roomId]` serves both types and branches on `roomType`: the retro join flow applies the **join policy** and hides the spectator toggle ([ADR-0013](0013-retro-permissions-extend-the-one-decision.md)). `/team/[teamId]` is the team page ([ADR-0008](0008-a-team-is-the-permanent-visibility-boundary.md)). No new primary-nav item.
- **Homepage.** Hero reframed as above with two CTAs, *Start estimating* → `/room/new` and *Start a retro* → `/retro/new`. A new "two ceremonies, one toolkit" section directly under the hero, one card per ceremony with its own CTA. `how-it-works` becomes tabbed per ceremony; `app-preview` shows both boards; use-cases, FAQ and the pricing section gain retro lines. Poker copy and screenshots are kept and re-scoped under their ceremony, never deleted. Retro screenshots are captured from the built board, so the copy is written first and the images land with the implementation.
- **`/features`** is one page with two anchored sections, `#planning-poker` and `#retro`, and its title drops "Planning Poker". The site default title becomes "Free Planning Poker & Retros Online | AgileKit"; the About page's "an open-source planning poker tool" sentence takes the position above. SEO strategy is out of scope, but metadata may not contradict the hero.
- **Dashboard sidebar** becomes Overview / **Planning poker** / **Retros** / Settings. Planning poker holds today's sessions list and analytics unchanged. Retros lists the retros the person attended, grouped by Team with teamless ones under "No team", and is where Teams are reached. Open action items live on the team page, not the dashboard, because they belong to a Team; retro analytics was later resolved as **retro facts** on the history row, never a report ([ADR-0024](0024-a-retro-shows-facts-and-never-a-measure-of-the-team.md)).
- **The team page** holds exactly: retro history in creation order, open action items carried across retros ([#265](https://github.com/spokvulcan/poker-planning/issues/265)), members and the rotatable invite link, the **retro defaults** panel (attribution, join policy, four permission levels), and *New retro*. Nothing else — the Team is minimal by decision. It is reachable from the Retros section, from the retro board's header (the "kept by Acme Squad" disclosure ADR-0008 already requires at write time doubles as the link), and from the user menu.
- **The claims register is part of the spec**, so it is not re-litigated in a marketing PR. *May say*: written and parallel, everyone at once; async collection before the meeting; permanent history, free, for every team; anonymous link-join; open source; no cameras required. *May not say*: any AI; a workspace or org product; that retros improve delivery or outcomes; that anonymity makes retros better; the meta-analytic effect sizes; "X% of action items never get done"; anything about pricing tiers for retro; that any retro number measures the team ([ADR-0024](0024-a-retro-shows-facts-and-never-a-measure-of-the-team.md)).
- **Words.** **Retro** in the UI, nav and dashboard; "retrospective" in titles, metadata and long-form copy; "ceremony" only in this repository's docs. "Session" is retired as a category word and survives only inside Planning poker.
