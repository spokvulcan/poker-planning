# A retro shows facts and never a measure of the team

**Status:** accepted — decided on [map #253](https://github.com/spokvulcan/poker-planning/issues/253) via [#280](https://github.com/spokvulcan/poker-planning/issues/280). Specified, not yet built. Amends [ADR-0014](0014-retro-is-the-second-ceremony-of-one-toolkit.md): the claims register gains a line.

Every retro tool with continuity eventually grows a dashboard, and the research behind this map says that is the feature most likely to break the ceremony it measures. `docs/research/retrospective-effectiveness.md` §5.3: Dingsøyr et al. observed teams toning down or removing critique once retro minutes became visible beyond the team, and "the moment retro history feeds analytics or an org-level view, candour is at risk — not at the moment of storage". The evidence for the numbers themselves is thinner than their popularity: no study measures action-item completion in software teams (§4.4, and Dingsøyr tried and could not), so "X% never get done" is already forbidden copy; Locke & Latham require feedback on progress *against each goal*, which is a per-action state and not a rate; and Lehtinen et al. found that the topics which recur across retros are the ones a team cannot fix by itself, so a recurrence chart reads as a team failing at what is in fact waste outside its control. The teardown (`docs/research/retro-tool-teardown.md`) finds only TeamRetro charting trends, on a separate paid health-check radar, and nobody charting card data across retros.

So: **a retro shows facts about itself and the Team shows counts, and nothing on either surface measures the team.** A history row, reused by the team page's history and the dashboard's Retros list, holds exactly what the `retros` row and the `retroActions` team index already store: name, format name, created date, attribution, resting stage, the coverage readout when a walk exists, and that retro's action counts (`open / done / dropped`, unowned). The team page carries one line over its open-actions list, "3 open · 12 done · 2 dropped across 14 retros". Card count appears only on the board, where the cards are already loaded. Everything is a live query; there is no snapshot, no counter and no analytics route for retro.

## Considered Options

- **Nothing beyond history and open actions** (rejected). Throws away information the rows already carry, and a team reading its history should know what each retro was without opening it.
- **Facts, not analytics** (chosen). Every number shown is one the board already shows; there is no aggregation across retros beyond action counts, no chart and no interpretation.
- **A retro analytics view beside poker's, with cross-retro numbers** (rejected). The Dingsøyr feature. It also has no honest content: the register may not claim outcomes, and the only cross-retro number with a constituency, the completion rate, is the one the evidence does not support.
- **Same view, but a team admin sees more** (rejected). The moment the admin sees more it is a manager's dashboard. Team role grants no room powers ([ADR-0008](0008-a-team-is-the-permanent-visibility-boundary.md)) and grants no reading powers here either.
- **A denormalised counter on the `retros` row** (rejected). Turns every card creation into a patch on the row every member subscribes to, the churn [ADR-0016](0016-a-retro-is-one-room-with-its-ceremony-state-beside-it.md) split the reads to avoid.
- **A per-retro snapshot on leaving `discuss`, [ADR-0007](0007-analytics-read-from-a-write-time-snapshot.md) style** (rejected). ADR-0007 existed because nine queries re-scanned votes; here the facts are stored in the shape they are read. It would also invent the finished state [ADR-0010](0010-a-retro-stage-projects-and-defaults-but-never-forbids.md) refuses.
- **"Insights" as the name** (rejected). An insight is an interpretation, which is what this feature withholds, and it is Parabol's name for its paid cross-meeting analytics. The word is **retro facts**.

## Consequences

- **Candidates judged.** Ships: action counts per retro; action counts across the team's history as counts, never a rate or percentage; coverage per retro, which is a fraction of what the team chose to discuss ([ADR-0023](0023-the-walk-covers-what-was-voted-for-and-a-person-raises-the-rest.md)). Never: cards per person or any participation figure, in both attribution modes, because computing it ranks members even where a human could read it off a named board; timebox adherence, because timeboxes are advisory and no stage entry time is stored; sentiment, explicitly including non-AI heuristics such as prompt-polarity ratios; format usage counts, because a format has no key ([ADR-0021](0021-a-custom-format-is-the-last-retros-format.md)). Out of the map's scope: theme recurrence across retros, with the Lehtinen reason recorded and no seam beyond the stored cluster name.
- **Standing refusals**, written into the spec so they are not re-litigated per PR: nothing per person in either attribution mode; nothing that ranks or orders members; nothing across teams or across the instance; nothing time-based from `rooms.lastActivityAt` ([ADR-0018](0018-the-activity-chokepoint-owns-the-clocks-precision.md)); no rate, percentage, score, streak, trend line or comparison to a previous retro; nothing derived from card text, word counts and keyword matches included; nothing derived from a retro that is not retained ([ADR-0019](0019-retention-follows-the-team-and-export-never-widens-access.md)).
- **Who sees it.** Team members only, the same view for admin and member. An attendee who is not in the Team sees the retro's own facts on its board and nothing cross-retro. Reads go through the same attribution and room-access projections as export.
- **The claims register** ([ADR-0014](0014-retro-is-the-second-ceremony-of-one-toolkit.md)) gains a line under *may not say*: that any retro number measures the team. In the UI, numbers are labelled as facts with a unit ("3 done · 2 open · 1 dropped", "7 of 10 covered"), never as a rate, score, health, streak or trend; no comparative copy such as "up from last time"; **no colour coding by value**, because a red "2 open" is a judgement; no sentence relating a number to team quality.
- **Placement.** Done and dropped action items are reachable only through their home retro's board; the team page's done and dropped counts are facts, not links, because a browsable done list is a team-page surface ADR-0014 excluded and an action item has one home ([ADR-0017](0017-an-action-item-has-one-home-and-carries-over-by-staying-open.md)). Dropped is shown, not hidden: a hole in the record is honest, the same rule as a deleted card.
- **Cost.** The history row reads fields on `retros` and an indexed count on `retroActions`; nothing scans cards across retros, so listing a team's history costs one row per retro plus one index range, with no cache to keep fresh.
